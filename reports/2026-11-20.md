# Daily Product Catalog Review

Date: 2026-11-20

### Daily Review: Product Catalog Project

1. **Accessibility (Keyboard & Screen Readers):** Ensure that interactive elements in your product grid—especially filter buttons, sort dropdowns, and "Add to Cart" triggers—are fully keyboard accessible. Add explicit `aria-label` attributes to icon-only buttons (like wishlist toggles or close modals) and manage focus properly when opening product detail drawers.

2. **Usability (Loading & Empty States):** Because Vite applications load rapidly, users might still experience a flash of missing content during data fetches. Implement skeleton loaders for the product cards and a robust empty state component with a "Reset Filters" action for when search queries yield zero results.

3. **Maintainability (Component Composition & Types):** Since you are using TypeScript, avoid inline type definitions for complex product data structures. Centralize your interfaces (e.g., `Product`, `FilterState`) in a dedicated `types/` directory, and break down monolithic catalog components into smaller, single-responsibility units like `ProductCard`, `ProductFilters`, and `Pagination`.
